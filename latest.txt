At the blockchain week summit, a lot of bitcoins, but an idea of using this for patient data:

🔒 1. Patientory
Blockchain used: Ethereum-based.

Tech details: Patient data is encrypted and stored off-chain, while blockchain handles access control and audit trails.

Token: Uses PTOY token for network access and services.

📂 2. Medicalchain
Blockchain used: Hyperledger Fabric (for permissioned access) + Ethereum (for tokens).

Data model: Off-chain data storage with hashes and permissions on-chain.

Use: Enables patients to grant temporary access to medical professionals.

💡 3. BurstIQ
Blockchain used: Proprietary blockchain framework tailored for healthcare compliance.

Purpose: Secure, compliant sharing of health data, identity, and consent.

Extra: Smart contracts used to define data-sharing rules.

🛡️ 4. Guardtime
Blockchain used: KSI (Keyless Signature Infrastructure), a proprietary blockchain-like system.

Government partner: Worked with Estonia on eHealth systems.

Focus: Integrity verification of health records, not just data sharing.

🌐 5. Solve.Care
Blockchain used: Ethereum-based, with smart contracts for coordination.

Platform: Decentralizes healthcare coordination (scheduling, benefits, data).

Token: SOLVE token used for payments and operations.
