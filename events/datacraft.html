Event Takeaways

Event 1 – Agent Security & LLM Risks

  • There may be security breaches in agent-based systems depending on how information is framed or prompted (e.g. agents requesting urgent salary transfers when messages are tagged as CEO demands).
  • This highlights the importance of testing agents and LLMs specifically for security vulnerabilities and prompt-based manipulation.
  • Robust evaluation frameworks are needed to assess agent behavior under adversarial, misleading, or high-pressure scenarios.

Event 2 – Digital Twins, Simulation, and Crisis Management

  • Computation costs can become extremely high at scale (e.g. a village-scale model with 200 × 200 × 10 GB of storage).
  • Digital twins can be effectively used for crisis management, such as modeling stationary flow direction and the spread of smoke or pollutants.
  • Having a simulation engineer on the team is essential to tailor sensors and integrate new data into digital twin simulations.
  • This insight is particularly useful for designing a team or consultancy focused on data acquisition, as most organizations already have domain specialists embedded in their departments.
