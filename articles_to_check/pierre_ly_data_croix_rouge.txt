Red Cross is 70000 volunteers and 20000 employees. The French Red cross works for, amongst others, street outreach, a small call center of 100 people to answer people in need, natural catastrophees on the territory such as Mayotte recently. There is a different entity which headquarter is in Geneva, the Red Cross Comittee for Conflict Zones, founded in 1963, for Switzerland being historically a neutral zone.
  Pierre Ly is today the Coordinator of Data and AI Innovations at French Red Cross. He started his career at the Ecole Polytechnique and continued with a master's at Supaero. He worked first at Thales and then at Dassault System in Engineering and identifies himself more as on the operational side and deploiement of technology than being a technical person. He started at the Red Cross as a volunteer and got hired for a small project of logistics of deliveries. During Covid he held a position of working against social isolation, for citizen mobilisation and vaccination for 10 million people (as Red Cross is also present in hospitals with nurses and in medico-social), amongst other things. He told me about his position and the state of the organisation in data.


"When we started, we worked on all tech-related topics without project management, in a reactive mode, being highly operational and responding to needs for the next day. We were 15 volunteers, we solved issues very quickly, having to plan things on the roadmap. It is now interesting for me to be making the connections with crisis topics and crisis management.
  When Covid stopped, I started working for the Ukraine crisis. We needed to solve the issue of entire TGVs getting in with refugees.
  We then needed people for another new project: new treatments. We recruted people and started working in V-cycle: scope of work, operational idea, technique, conception and then testing and deploiement. In this process, you then have more and more complex needs to implement in the back and forth with users and testers. I can as well work with the IT department to drive the specialist we contract to explain to them what we want.
  I also worked on the Olympics.
  There is still no real organization for a data and AI unit and we are only two or three to speak this language fluently. Yet it is going to grow in the next months to year.
  In order to reduce the administrative charge, we use chatbots. We have thought about a tool for crisis scenario simulation for first-aid workers. As background, we have the idea of piloting with data with the volunteers. Another subject is the piloting through figures which is not the case right now and should arrive soon.
   Building a data project is not so easy as it is a big organization and we have a certain inertia and a certain lack of resources. We need funds, as the other activities at Red Cross. We regularly check the postings on tech for good or AI for good, we can propose projects to sponsors, philanthropists and tech companies. We can also, more concretely, partner with schools in hackatons, such as 42.
  I have recently participated in the DataCraft awards and we'll be there on June 19th for the ceremony.
  R&D capacity is high and we can go fast on AI, there is a huge demand and the subjects crisis-oriented are many at the international level. There are several categories: training, emergency operations, distribution, shelter, cash, transfer, water sanitation, logistics, and so on.
  For our operations, we come and help the local Red Cross or Red Crescent. We support operationals that are already there. This year I had a training in management for 10 days to be validated to be a coordinator on information management and I would like to go on the field to understand better the needs, for at least month and a half, that is the level above volunteer that stay three weeks. Otherwise the turnover is too high. I am 33 and I live in Lyon with my girlfriend.
  Programs of development, in emergency, are normed by humanitarian standards, UN agencies and big NGOs are united for sphere standards. There are iso-norms for humanitarian, for instance on distribution: humane, impartial, neutral, water for the week instead of just a bottle, and so on. It is very normed contrary to national associations and adapts to needs. Humanitarian is standardized and protocoled as an opex. So we have a radically different mission than humanitarian."
